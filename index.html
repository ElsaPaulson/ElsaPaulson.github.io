About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of North Texas, working in software testing and automation under Dr. Renée C. Bryce. My research focuses on test suite reduction, Playwright-based web testing, and using machine learning and reinforcement learning to identify redundant test cases while preserving coverage.

Before my Ph.D., I worked for several years as a Quality Analyst and Test Automation Engineer, building and maintaining frameworks using tools such as Selenium, Cypress, and Playwright. I enjoy bridging research and practice by turning ideas into tools that make testing more efficient and reliable.
